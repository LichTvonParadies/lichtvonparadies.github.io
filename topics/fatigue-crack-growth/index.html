Fatigue crack growth

A hybrid XFEM-calibrated analytical framework for efficient fatigue crack growth prediction

Sheng Zhang, Enhui Zhang, John Cookson, Pu Huang, Wenyi Yan
International Journal of Fatigue

The XCA framework periodically calibrates a through-thickness geometric factor using stationary 3D XFEM analyses, then integrates crack-growth laws analytically. Tests on two railway steels examine constant-amplitude and single-overload loading, including the effect of out-of-plane constraint.

XCA 框架通过静态三维 XFEM 周期标定厚度方向几何因子，再以解析方式积分裂纹扩展规律。研究以两种铁路钢的紧凑拉伸试样验证恒幅及单次过载工况，并分析面外约束对过载迟滞的影响。

Fatigue crack growth

A unified framework for full-lifecycle fatigue crack evolution in hollow-section structures

Sheng Zhang, Enhui Zhang, John Cookson, Pu Huang, Wenyi Yan
Engineering Fracture Mechanics

A three-stage framework tracks a surface crack through wall breakthrough and subsequent through-thickness growth in a railway drawbar. Periodic XFEM calibration, global-local modelling and analytical Paris-law integration support efficient service-load simulations; the quantitative results are specific to the studied rectangular hollow-section wall.

采用三阶段拓扑描述、整体—局部建模和 XFEM 周期标定解析积分，连续追踪铁路拉杆表面裂纹、壁厚突破及贯穿后的演化。定量结果针对所研究的矩形空心拉杆平直壁面，不是普适尺寸阈值或通用检修标准。
