In-trainNet: A Two-Step Data-Driven Framework for Enhancing Railway In-Train Forces Monitoring

Sheng Zhang, Wenyi Yan
Advanced Engineering Informatics

In-trainNet combines multi-task pre-training with transfer learning to estimate forces at multiple couplers and adapt to different train configurations. The study evaluates simulation-based accuracy and computational performance, while identifying limitations in measured data and absolute errors.

In-trainNet 通过多任务预训练和迁移学习，在多个车钩与不同列车配置之间扩展纵向力估计。其证据来自列车动力学仿真与基准数据，实际应用仍需关注现场数据不足及部分配置的绝对误差。

A Machine Learning Approach for Predicting Railway In-Train Forces from ATO Measurements

Sheng Zhang, Pu Huang, Tim Constable, Wenyi Yan
Advances in Dynamics of Vehicles on Roads and Tracks III

This IAVSD 2023 conference chapter presents an SA-CNN approach to estimating in-train force histories from ATO-related data. It explicitly points readers to the 2024 Advanced Engineering Informatics journal paper for detailed results.

这篇 IAVSD 2023 会议论文章节介绍利用 ATO 相关数据和 SA-CNN 估计列车纵向力的方法，并明确指向 2024 年期刊论文获取详细研究。会议年份、首次在线年份和卷册出版年份应分别记录。

A data-driven approach for railway in-train forces monitoring

Sheng Zhang, Pu Huang, Wenyi Yan
Advanced Engineering Informatics

A self-attention causal convolutional neural network estimates coupler force histories from simulated automatic-train-operation measurements. It offers a computationally efficient alternative to repeatedly running longitudinal train dynamics simulations for the studied train configuration.

利用纵向列车动力学仿真生成训练数据，以自注意力因果卷积神经网络估计车钩纵向力时程。研究验证了指定列车配置下的计算效率与仿真一致性，尚未以实测车钩力完成现场验证。
